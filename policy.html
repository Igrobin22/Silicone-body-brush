Product & care

What comes in the set?

The set includes a long handle back scrubber, bath body brush, scalp massager, and hexagonal face brush.

Can I use the brushes every day?

Yes, the silicone bristles are designed for regular use. Use light pressure, especially on the face and scalp.

How do I clean the brushes?

Rinse thoroughly with warm water after each use and leave them in an open-air place to dry.

Is the face brush separate from the body brush?

Yes. The face brush is smaller and softer for delicate facial areas.

Are Grey, Black, and Pink separate products?

Yes. Each color is treated as its own set choice, but every color includes the same four tools.

Ordering & payment

Can I order different colors together?

Yes. Add each Grey, Black, or Pink set to your cart, then pay for them together at checkout.

How do I start an order?

Choose a set and quantity, add it to your cart, then enter your UAE delivery details and continue to secure card payment.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

No. PureForm accepts card payment only, with delivery timing confirmed before dispatch.

Delivery & returns

How much is delivery?

AED 10 per order across the UAE, shown before card payment.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on city, courier availability, and stock. PureForm confirms timing before dispatch.

Can I return the set?

Unused sets may be returned within 15 days. Opened personal care products may only be eligible if damaged or incorrect. Read the return policy for details.

What details should I send for support?

Include your name, phone number, delivery city, product color, number of sets, and a short description of the question. Contact support.

Store policies

Last updated October 17, 2026

Delivery

Shipping & card payment

Delivery costs AED 10 per order across the United Arab Emirates. This fee is shown in your cart and checkout before card payment. Orders are prepared after payment is confirmed. Delivery timing depends on your city, courier availability, and stock, and is confirmed before dispatch.

Shipping Info

City and timing confirmation

PureForm does not treat delivery timing as confirmed until the customer shares a delivery city and contact number. If delivery is unavailable for a location, support will explain the available alternatives before the customer confirms the order.

Returns

15-day return window

Unused products may be returned within 15 days of delivery if they are in original condition and packaging. Because this is a personal care product, opened or used items may not be returnable unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

Return Process

How to request a return

Contact support with the order name, phone number, delivery city, reason for return, and clear photos where relevant. Return pickup, shipping cost, or replacement details are confirmed by support before the item is accepted back.

Refunds

Inspection before refund

Returned items are checked before a refund or replacement is approved. Approved refunds are processed back through the original card payment method when possible, or by another agreed method if support confirms it.

Privacy

Customer data

PureForm collects only the details needed to process orders, respond to customer messages, and improve the shopping experience. Customer information should not be sold to unrelated third parties.

Terms

Website use

Product details, pricing, offers, and availability may change. Customers should review the final order information before confirming a purchase. Website content may not be copied or reused without permission.

Support

Questions before ordering

For questions about product use, delivery, returns, or order status, use the contact page and include the order name, phone number, and delivery city where relevant.

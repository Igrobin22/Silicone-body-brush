All questions & answers
What's included in a set?

One back scrubber, one body brush, one scalp massager and one face brush. Grey, Black and Pink sets contain the same four tools.

Where do you deliver?

PureForm delivers within the United Arab Emirates. Enter your delivery address at checkout, or contact support to check timing for your city.

How do I pay?

Checkout accepts card payments through the hosted payment gateway. PureForm never stores your card number or security code. Cash on delivery is not available.

Can I return my set?

Unused sets in their original condition and packaging can be returned within 15 days. Read the return policy for the full details.

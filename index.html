All questions & answers
What's included in a set?

One back scrubber, one body brush, one scalp massager and one face brush. Grey, Black and Pink sets contain the same four tools.

Where do you deliver?

PureForm delivers within the United Arab Emirates. Estimated delivery: 2–3 working days. Enter your delivery address at checkout, or contact support if you need your order by a specific date.

How do I pay?

Checkout accepts card payments through the hosted payment gateway. PureForm never stores your card number or security code. Cash on delivery is not available.

Can I mix set colors?

Yes. Choose any combination of Grey, Black and Pink. Buy two or more sets for free UAE delivery; one set has an AED 10 delivery fee.
